# Data

Raw article text is **not** committed (spec §6.1). Everything under `data/` is regenerated by the
commands below, except `data/gold/` (extraction gold set) and `data/bench/` (benchmark questions
and annotations), which are committed.

## Source

Public Apple support articles from `support.apple.com` (en-us), discovered through the site's
own sitemap index (`/en-us/sitemaps/sitemap-index-en-us.xml`, the `ac` sitemap lists ~1,900
numeric article URLs).

Collection rules, enforced in `src/fixgraph/ingest/scrape.py`:

- `robots.txt` is fetched and obeyed for every URL (`urllib.robotparser`).
- At most 1 request per second; a descriptive User-Agent with a contact address.
- Raw HTML is cached in `data/raw/html/<article_id>.html`; reruns fetch only missing pages.

## Regenerate

```powershell
uv run fixgraph ingest scrape            # ~35 min at 1 req/s; --limit N / --dry-run available
uv run fixgraph ingest parse --target 1000
uv run fixgraph ingest chunk
```

| Step | Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| scrape | `data/raw/html/*.html`, `data/raw/article_urls.json` | cached, resumable |
| parse | `data/corpus/articles.parquet` | `article_id, url, title, summary, product_tags, os_versions_mentioned, last_updated, sections_json` |
| chunk | `data/corpus/chunks.parquet` | `chunk_id = {article_id}:{section_idx}:{chunk_idx}`, char offsets into the canonical article text |

### Corpus selection

`parse` keeps up to `--target` articles about the target families (iPhone, Apple Watch, AirPods,
Mac, iPad) ranked by a transparent keyword score (`src/fixgraph/ingest/select.py`):
troubleshooting cues in the title (+2), a target family in the title (+2), step lists (+0.5),
reasonable length (+1). Legal, security-release, enterprise/developer and pro-app pages
(Logic, Final Cut, MainStage, ...) are excluded. Ties break by article id, so selection is
deterministic.

### Chunking

Structure-aware (spec §6.3): one chunk per section; sections under 200 tokens merge forward
while the group stays under 500; oversized sections split at paragraph/step boundaries with a
one-unit overlap. Chunk text is always an exact substring of the canonical article text
(`ingest.chunk.article_text`), so offsets are verifiable. Rerunning produces identical IDs
(property-tested).

## Secondary data (M5)

Kaggle "Customer Support on Twitter" filtered to `@AppleSupport`: used only as style examples
for rephrasing benchmark questions. Download it yourself from Kaggle into
`data/external/twcs.csv`; it is not redistributed here.
